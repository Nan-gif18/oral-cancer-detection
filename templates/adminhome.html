Platform Overview

Welcome to the Oral Cancer Detection Admin Panel. Here, administrators can efficiently monitor and manage hospitals, users, screening camps, and educational content. The platform provides a centralized hub to ensure smooth coordination between hospitals and users, oversee complaints and feedback, and track the effectiveness of early detection initiatives. Administrators can also review and approve hospital registrations, organize outreach programs, and ensure the platform provides a seamless and secure healthcare experience for everyone involved.

Hospital Management

Monitor pending hospital registrations, approve verified hospitals, and maintain updated hospital information for users seeking care.

User & Complaint Oversight

View registered users, track complaints, and respond to feedback to ensure a transparent and supportive healthcare environment.

Screening Camps & Appointments

Organize and monitor screening camps, manage appointment slots, and ensure smooth communication between hospitals and patients.

Educational Resources

Curate and manage educational materials to raise awareness about oral cancer, early detection methods, and preventive care for the community.
